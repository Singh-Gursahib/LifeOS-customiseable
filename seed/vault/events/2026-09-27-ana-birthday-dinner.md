# Ana's Sixteenth Birthday

Took the morning bus home for [[ana|Ana]]'s sixteenth birthday.

[[mom|Mom]] made her lasagna — the one she only makes for birthdays. [[dad|Dad]] gave a
speech that started as a toast and turned into five minutes on her robotics team's
regional result. I gave her a soldering kit, which she opened and immediately started
reading the manual for at the table.

Bus ticket $22, gift $38 — see [[expenses]]. Back on the late bus; asleep before the
first stop.
